Roblox DOORS

DOORS is a Trial and Error horror game made by the developers LSplash and Redibles based on Rooms on Roblox by nicorocks5555, which is based on Spookys Jumpscare Mansion. It was released on August 10th 2022.

The main game takes place in a Hotel with many entities you have to survive in different ways and 100 randomly generated rooms you have to go through
