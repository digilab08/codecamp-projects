The Hotel

Main

Name Appearance Mechanic
Moonlight Blue Light Helps you by highlighting stuff, saving you from entities, emitting sounds near important objects and gives survival tips if you die.
Seek Humanoid made of black goo with one eye Chases you through specific Rooms. Appears 2 times in a Run
The Figure Tall monster made of reddish flesh with a big mouth for a face. It is blind and walks around to find you. Runs instantly towards loud sounds.

Common

Name Appearance Mechanic
Rush Gray smiling face with a lot of black smoke The lights flicker when it arrives. It then rushes through the room. You have to hide out of sight or in a closet to survive.
Screech A floating head covered in black gooo with glowing white eyes. It only appears in dark rooms. When it whispers "Pst.." you hvae to look at it.
Eyes A purple fog with very many eyes It appears in a room and you then have to avoid eye contact
Dupe A grinning face with 2 long arms Sometimes there are multiple doors with a number. If you do not remember at what door you are and pick the wrong one you get damaged.
Hide A red "Face" with many eyes If you hide in a hiding spot for too long you get damaged and kicked out of it. After that you temporarily can not enter one.
Void A black void If you play with other people and get left behind it brings you back to the group and damages you.

Uncommon

Name Appearance Mechanic
Ambush A white, eyeless face acompanied by green light If you hear a loud scream in the distance then it appears. It behaves similiarly to Rush but it rebounds 2-3 times. That means you have to pay attention if it is really gone.
Halt A teal Ghost-like entity When you enter its room, The Dining hall you have to go through it. When it tells you to turn around you have to go the other way. This gets repeated until you reach the end.
Sally A child-like entity with glowing eyes and teeth A room before she appears you hear a laugh. When you enter the next room you have to give her an item or a toy that randomly is somewhere in the room. Failing to do so or giving an item reduces your max health
Timothy A small greenish spider It hides in drawers and other containers. When you open one it jumps at you doing 5 damage. It is non-fatal but can cause some scares
Snare A brown piece of dirt with many holes on the ground If you step into one it holds you in place for a bit with its spikes. It is non-fatal, but in combination with other entities it can cause some troubles

Rare

Name Appearance Mechanic
Jack A white, shadowed Face It can hide in closets or behind doors to scare you. If you encounter it behind a Door it turns the whole room into a bloody red
Dread A white "Face" with many holes and mouths If you stay in the same room for 5 minutes the screen goes gray and it appears. It is barely visible and slowly approaches your location. If you enter the next room it dissappears.
Weirdo A humanoid shadow It appears rarely in the next room for just a second before dissapearing
