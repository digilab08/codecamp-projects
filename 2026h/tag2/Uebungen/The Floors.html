The Floors

The Main Floors

The Hotel

The Hotel is the first and main Floor. It has 100 Rooms and has 20 entities, 4 of them being friendly. The appearance of the rooms commonly have carpets and paintings and are made out of wood.

Important Rooms

  • Room 0: The Reception
  • Room 50: The Library
  • Room 53 to 59/Jeffs Shop
  • Room 60: The Archives Entrance
  • Room 67: The Infirmary
  • Room 89: The Courtyard
  • Room 90-99: The Greenhouse
  • Room 90 and 99: The Outdoors Entrance
  • Room 100: The Electrical Room

The Mines

The Mines are the second Floor in the game. It also has 100 rooms and starts at room 100 and ends at 200. There are 25 entities and 2 hazards, 5 of them being friendly The appearance is cave-like due to the fact these are mines. The walls are mostly stone and there are commonly wooden supports.

Important Rooms

  • Room 100: The Adit
  • Room 150: The Nest
  • Room 152: The Stairwell Entrance
  • Room 154 to 162: Jeffs Shop
  • Room 200: The Dam

The Subfloors

The Backdoor

The Backdoor is a Subfloor that is placed before The Hotel. It has 50 rooms going from -50 to 0. There are 4 entities, 1 of them being friendly and 1 hazard. The whole Subfloor is on a Timer so you have to be fast to complete it. Some rooms contain levers to increase the time. The appearance is similiar to The Hotel, being mostly made out of wood. The difference is that everything looks much older and some parts of the rooms missing completely. It gives the feeling that this place has been forgotten

Important Rooms

  • Room -51: The Back Entry
  • Room -50: Guaranteed Gate Room
  • Room -01: The Altar Room

The Archives

The Archives is a Replacement for The Rooms. It has 26 Sectors going from A to Z. Each Sector contains 50 Rooms totalling in 1300 rooms. There are 11 entities, 3 of them being friendly and 1 hazard. This is currently the largest Subfloor in the game. Due to that there is a way to skip Sectors if you have enough in-game Money. The Archives look like an Office building with many desks and whiteboards. There is also some sort of day and night cycle which is 12 in-game hours long. 5AM to 9AM is night. The rest is day.

Important Rooms

  • Room A-000: The Atrium
  • Room A-050, B-050 etc...: Sector End
  • Room C-050, D.050 etc...: Emergency Exits
  • Room S-050: The Archival Storage
  • In Sector J: The Cafeteria
  • Room Z-050: The Archives End

Sector Variants

  • Bad Ventilation: Limited Visibility, much fog
  • Overgrown: Some rooms have grass on the ground, green lighting
  • Pizza Party: Some Rooms have Pizzas on the tables, colourful lighting.
  • Room Anomaly: Some Rooms do not make much sense and look like htey clip into each other. Purple lighting
  • Managers Office: Area where the Bossfight happens. After around 8 doors everything is back to normal.

The Outdoors

The Outdoors have 35 Rooms but those are mostly very large. There are no Door numbers, instead Meters are used (e.g: 2500m left). There are 11 entities,3 being friendly and 1 being neutral and 1 hazard. The Outdoors are currently the only Floor where you are not in a room. Instead you are actually outside for once. This Subfloor resembles a garden and has much plantlife. There are also many cabins in some rooms.

Important Rooms

  • At 0m/ Small Courtyard
  • At 1200m: The Clearing
  • At 2100m: The Mill
  • At 2250m: The Hedgemaze

The Stairwell

The Stairwell is the currently most recent Subfloor in the game. You have to climb up 100 landings There are 7 entities,2 being friendly and 2 hazard. The Stairwell is very dark and much spookier than the other Floors. Every once in a while there is a Maze-like landing where you can find salvage which you can turn into scraps with the Grinders This Subfloor is infested with something called "Meld" which is some type of mold that spreads, which you have to burn sometimes.

Important Rooms

  • Landing 0: The Bottom floor
  • Landing 100: Cobblers Shop
